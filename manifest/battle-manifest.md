Grím in the crown's service: hunting the Landless, policing the ban, learning what the king's verse costs to carry. The crown's recording skald rides with every kingsman column — the route's standing witness motif.

## Chapter 2 — Outlaw (PLANNED, route-split, Y+1 spring–autumn)

Grím as skógarmaðr, killable on sight: the hunt, the Wolves, the Landless — and the arithmetic of staying a rumor.

| id | chapter | order | route tags | one-line brief | height gimmick | witness setup |
