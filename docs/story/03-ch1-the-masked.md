## Chapter Epigraph

*Composed, Hávamál-flavor, fornyrðislag: two stressed alliterating syllables per half-line, no rhyme (C-TONE-01/02).*

> Winter weighs    the worth of men;
> the fell-wind finds    each failing seam.
> Wear what mask    the mood requires —
> fire will find    the face beneath.
>
> Guard your grið    where great men smile:
> a king's kindness    is a knife in cloth.
> Blood binds deeper    than the bar of law;
> no verse can void    what the vein keeps.

## Chapter-Open — the Skald

**[STAGE: the hall, ~Y+300. The fire is fed — by the SKALD's own hand; no thrall comes, none ever comes. He is never named, now or ever (C-FRAME-04). The mead in his horn has not warmed all night — mark it, and let no one remark it (C-WORLD-02). Present tense, second person, to the benches (C-TONE-03).]**

> **SKALD:** Hold your cups. The first winter, then.
>
> A coastal hold no saga had ever needed: forge-smoke, herring-racks, a tide that keeps the only calendar that matters. And a smith's fostered boy with half a face and a whole silence — nobody, and glad of it, for he had learned young what being *named* had cost the last of his blood to bear a name. The saga keeps that winter like a scar. It is the winter the word found him.
>
> Mark, as I sing, who is *watching* in every scene I give you. The saga is built of nothing else. Where no one watched, I have nothing; and where I have something, someone watched — and before this night is old you will learn that neither of those is the same thing as the truth.
>
> It begins, as it ends, with smoke on the tideline.
